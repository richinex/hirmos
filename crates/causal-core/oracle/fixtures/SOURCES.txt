slsqp.json was regenerated on 2026-09-25 by running oracle/slsqp.py under
SciPy 1.17.1 and NumPy 2.5.3 in the python:3.12-slim image. The retained copy
had been produced on macOS, where NumPy links Accelerate rather than OpenBLAS;
the two disagree on the deliberately infeasible "incompatible" case, whose
least-squares subproblem is rank deficient. Under the pinned Linux image SciPy
returns 1.9999999999986093 at maxiter=1, which this solver reproduces to every
digit, so the previously recorded discrepancy was in the fixture rather than in
the kernel. Regenerate with Docker to reproduce.

Two comparisons in checked_slsqp_constraints_and_limits_match_scipy are scoped
rather than exact, with the reasons stated at each site:
- "incompatible" at maxiter=100 aborts on a line-search failure (status 8) over
  that rank-deficient subproblem. The iteration it gives up on follows the
  floating-point path, not the algorithm: 9 under the pinned image, 20 on
  macOS, 14 here. The status, the point and its gradient are still checked, the
  last two within 1e-11 instead of 2e-13.
- "mixed" solves to x = (2, -1), where the equality row and both bounds are
  active: three constraint gradients in two dimensions, so the dual is a
  one-parameter family. SciPy reports 3 and this solver reports 1, and both
  satisfy the Karush-Kuhn-Tucker conditions with the signs each bound requires.
  The primal solution, objective, gradient and status match exactly.
